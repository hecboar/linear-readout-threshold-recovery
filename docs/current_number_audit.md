### Known limitations of this audit

- It covers E5 only. E1-E4 and E6 macros are checked for existence and traceability by
  `scripts/check_manuscript_numbers.py`, not recomputed here.
- It cannot detect an error that is present in both the raw records and the manuscript,
  i.e. a bug in the experiment rather than in the reporting. Those are addressed by the
  protocol audit in `current_claim_audit.md`.
